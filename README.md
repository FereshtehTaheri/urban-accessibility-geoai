# Urban Accessibility & Explainable GeoAI

A research portfolio focused on **urban accessibility, spatial inequality, and explainable GeoAI**, with applications to urban service accessibility in Tehran.

## Overview

This repository presents selected methods, analytical workflows, and reproducible examples developed through my doctoral research in Urban Planning.

The research examines how spatial opportunities for accessing urban services relate to residents' experienced accessibility, and how spatial analysis and explainable machine-learning methods can support more equitable urban planning.

## Research Themes

- Urban accessibility
- Spatial inequality
- Urban service provision
- Gender and overlapping inequalities
- Spatial analysis
- Explainable GeoAI
- Responsible AI in urban planning

## Main Research Components

### Urban Accessibility Conversion Loss (UACL)

UACL is a spatial diagnostic framework developed to examine differences between network-based potential accessibility and residents' experienced access to urban services.

The analysis combines multi-service accessibility measures with survey-based assessments across Tehran's 22 districts.

### Explainable GeoAI

Machine-learning models are used to investigate factors associated with experienced urban accessibility.

The analytical workflow includes:

- Python-based data analysis
- Spatial and survey predictors
- Geographically structured model validation
- SHAP-based model interpretation
- Model performance evaluation
- Fairness-oriented subgroup assessment
